About Me

A little about me

Biography; Hi, I'm Zero — an 20-year-old self-taught developer from Indonesia. I'm the creator of Project Luna, a long-term Minecraft Bedrock project focused on expanding the game with new progression systems, quality-of-life improvements, custom gameplay mechanics, and story-driven content. My main focus is Minecraft Bedrock Add-On development, especially designing and building gameplay systems. I enjoy creating combat mechanics, skill systems, progression systems, custom world generation, and quality-of-life features that make the game feel more engaging. I work independently across different parts of the Add-On development process, from Script APIs and complex JSON structures to custom entities, models, blocks, items, and gameplay mechanics. Core Capabilities; • Combat Mechanics & Gameplay Systems • Skill & Progression Systems • Custom World Generation • Quality-of-Life Features • Minecraft Bedrock Add-On Development • Custom Entities, Models, Blocks, & Items OPEN FOR COMMISSIONS!
